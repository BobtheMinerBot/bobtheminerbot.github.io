The problem

Rust never sleeps
in a marine climate.

Salt and humidity reach the steel inside your concrete. The steel corrodes, expands, and pushes the surface off from the inside. Left alone it moves from an eyesore to a structural problem, and to falling debris.

  • We cut back to sound concrete, no cosmetic patching over rust.
  • Exposed steel is treated so the corrosion stops instead of returning.
  • Repair materials are chosen for salt exposure, not for a dry climate.

Questions

The things owners ask first.

What exactly is spalling?
Concrete chipping, flaking, or peeling away from the surface. In the Keys the usual cause is the steel reinforcement inside corroding and expanding, which cracks the concrete off from within.
Do you handle the permits and engineering?
Yes. We prepare engineering plans, submit permit applications and renewals, coordinate with Monroe County and municipal offices, and schedule inspections through close out.
What areas do you serve?
We are based in Marathon, FL 33050 and work throughout the Florida Keys.
How long will my project take?
It depends on how far the damage has gone and on the permitting path for your property. We give you a timeline with your written quote rather than a number over the phone.
What does the free quote include?
A written scope of the repair or build, the plans and permits it will require, and the sequence of work. It is free and carries no obligation.
Do you only do concrete work?
No. We are a full service general contractor. Alongside spalling repair and concrete resurfacing we take on remodeling, impact windows and doors, new construction, foundations, siding, and fencing.
Can you help with drainage or a swale?
Yes. Swales are often required by local government so stormwater is managed on the property. We handle site work and drainage as part of a build or on their own.
Can I see work you have finished?
Yes. The projects page has 20 photographed jobs across spalling repair, resurfacing, new construction, foundations, remodeling, siding, and fencing.
